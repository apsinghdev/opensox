## what is indie hacking?

popularized by indie hackers like [levelsio](https://x.com/levelsio), [mark lou](https://x.com/marclou), and [tony dinh](https://x.com/tdinh_me), indie hacking means building your own products, services, distribution, or all of these, and making money from it.

## why indie hacking?

there are many reasons why people choose indie hacking over a job or a traditional business, but my favorite is this:

indie hacking allows freedom.

in indie hacking you are not reporting to any board of shareholders or a boss. you do things on your own. whatever, wherever, whenever, with whomever you want. you have absolute freedom over your things.

also, since AI came into the picture, the barrier to becoming an indie hacker has dropped, because now you don't need a team or thousands of dollars to build a product.

a new wave has started, where most people are choosing indie businesses and making a good living.
