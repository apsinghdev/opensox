what do i need to know first before joining Opensox Pro?

this is the most common question i get regarding Opensox Pro.

most members in Opensox Pro join when they are just starting to learn programming, and with constant feedback, weekly meetings and Pro/Pro+ features they progress faster in Open Source (GSoC, LFX and Commercial Open Source companies).

Opensox Pro is built for absolute beginners so that the sooner you join, the earlier you avoid the beginner mistakes and the earlier you grow in the right direction.

obviously, if you are an experienced engineer or already know a lot in tech, you have a plus point.

personally, i suggest people start to do things before feeling ready as we never feel ready.

but again, i won't say that you have to join it now to make you sign up anyhow.

you can take your time and join whenever you feel like it.

you can ping me at [opensoxlabs@gmail.com](mailto:opensoxlabs@gmail.com) in case you have any questions!
