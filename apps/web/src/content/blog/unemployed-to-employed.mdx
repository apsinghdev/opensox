"am 2023 cse grad.....3 4 year gap after college still jobless...I know mern stack please guide me what to learn"

first decide the direction. where you wanna go? there're two steps of it.

1. decide the kind of companies you want to work for.
2. how much of salary you wanna make? (ive explained this part in details [here](/blog/never-underprice-yourself) so ill be focusing on the first one in article)

there're mainly 4 types of companies.

1. very early stage (pre seed)
2. early stage (seed to series B)
3. established startups (series C to series X)
4. MNCs (FAANG)

my target was to work for type-2 companies. coz these companies pay really well, stable enough, and money part in these indicate good founders and future. mostly.

now ask "what kind of engineers do the companies ive selected expect?"

to know this, you can DM some ppl on twitter who're working there, do the google search, watch any interviews avl on yt.

my fav is the first one.

if you're chosen type-1 to type-3 companies, you need to be good at [psycho level job hunting](/blog/psycho-level-job-hunting).

for the type-4, im not the right person. you can check striver.
