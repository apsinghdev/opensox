for me, build in public simply means "the best source of exposure".

here, exposure means "knowing what's possible."

because if you don't know what's possible, no matter how talented, smart or hard working you are, you'll end up at Infosys.

but saying "exposure" alone doesn't give a clear picture of "why" build-in-public.

so here're some concrete reasons with examples:

## 1. connections

in what world would a person who is most likely a millionaire chat in a google meet with a guy who lives in rural India?

i thought the same but this thing happened when i asked striver (founder of takeuforward) for some help regarding setting up a company, running it and managing it.

![1:1 with Striver](/assets/blog/striver-meet.png)

this is just one example.

ive talked to literal millionaires, multimillionaires, scientists, nuclear physicists, rocket scientists, founders and CTOs of many companies.

![Anton Osika from Lovable](/assets/blog/lovable-founder.png)

all this happened because i built in public.

## 2. you build your personal brand.

you see mr. beast (or your fav creator) on a thumbnail you immediately click on that video.

not always because his videos are great.

but because he's a brand. he has a face value.

that's what you build when you build in public.

ppl start recognizing your face and that's where your game starts.

## 3. universal application

tell me one thing where being popular doesn't help you?

from getting internships to jobs to initial customers to investors to buyers to even a life partner.

the applications of build in public are universal and evergreen.

Vinny Madan got hired through twitter

![Vinny Madan got hired through Twitter](/assets/blog/vinny-madan.png)

i got my first customer through twitter

![Opensox first sale through Twitter](/assets/blog/first-sale.png)

this dude found true love

![Found love on Twitter](/assets/blog/found-love.png)

## 4. build distribution — for unlimited experiments

when you have your online presence, an audience, you can experiment with many products because you already have a distribution.

building something is easy. getting the customers is incredibly hard.

but as you already have a distribution, you can build a product and get paid customers from day one.

## 5. ai can't copy this.

you can build a product using ai but you can't build trust using ai.

that's the hard part.

and that's why indie hackers, business owners start building the distribution from day one because they know that this doesn't have any shortcuts.

## 6. social credibility

when you build your online presence, social credibility is a byproduct of it.

when you look for a job, when you build a product, when you try to get help related to anything, your connections will vouch for you.

that's the result of sharing authentic content.

once my father had to go through a surgery and i was finding it a bit hard to get an appointment at AIIMS.

i posted on twitter and within 1 hour, i got more than 100+ DMs and one of them got me an appointment.

that was all! thanks for reading.
