in one year of indie hacking, ive been able to build multiple sources of income that pay my bills and most importantly allow me to do things i love to do without worrying about the rent of the next month or worrying about layoffs.

this is what ive built so far (on november 2026) since september 2025:

- opensox pro: profitable since start. <span class="blog-highlight">$1k-$2k/mo</span>
- youtube/twitter collabs: <span class="blog-highlight">$500-$700/mo</span> (as i don't accept much sponsor so sometimes it's <span class="blog-highlight">$0</span>)
- youtube ads revenue: <span class="blog-highlight">~$100/mo</span>
- youtube membership: <span class="blog-highlight">7</span> paid members so far. <span class="blog-highlight">$10-$20/mo</span>
- amazon affiliates: <span class="blog-highlight">~$50</span>

these numbers may not be fascinating to you as you may have seen ppl boasting "<span class="blog-highlight">$10k/mo</span>", "<span class="blog-highlight">1M MRR</span>", etc but for me the point of indie hacking was the freedom of being able to do things i want instead of completing jira tickets that ive achieved in the first year of starting only.

hopefully i'll cross <span class="blog-highlight">$10k/mo</span> this year.
