how to be an engineer?

a true engineer has two things under the hood in my opinion.

1. first principles
2. fundamentals

## first principles

it's a way to think and question any subject, topic or thing.

it lets you ask questions intuitively so that you actually know and understand about that particular topic/subject/thing.

it's the most fundamental skill for an engineer. you can build ur whole engineering empire on top of that.

ive explained the first principle and its applications in this [blog](/blog/first-principles) and [video](https://youtu.be/qLulWceLfMs?si=oTSzzsI6TNU1_91I).

## fundamentals

always remember "everything is a computer"

computer networks, memory, DBMS, operating system, basic maths.

these are the most primitive fundamentals everyone should know and understand.

you don't have to be cracked in these but you should be able to take a high level abstraction, let's say - APIs and then be able to co-relate it with the computer fundamentals.

just 3 hrs of chatgpting (for each) these with this [first principle prompt](https://github.com/apsinghdev/orgasm-prompts/blob/main/ivc/prompts.md#refined-version) will get you an idea of these.

also one way to be good at fundamentals is that always understand WHY part of everything.

once you do everything will be in your mind forever. no need for a revision.

TIP: whenever you learn somthing complex or a big enough context, always use a document. to write your questions, answers (in short. for reference only).

coz when you write the dots in brain connect.
