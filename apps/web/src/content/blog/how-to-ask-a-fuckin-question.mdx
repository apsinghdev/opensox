how to ask a fuckin question?

if your question is a paragraph-long life story, nobody's gonna answer it. not me. not a maintainer. not that famous person you're trying to reach.

characteristics of a good question:

- very short (not more than 3 lines)
- only 1-3 questions
- no/very short intro (just one line)
- well spaced lines
- to the point
- no jargons
- no obvious (and not needed) details mentioned
- no desperateness
- don't ask a question to ask a question
- ask this — "can i google/chatgpt it?"
- don't write 7 lines appreciating me
- don't mention "my friends are making it in life and travelling with their cute girlfriends"
- don't cry in it like "im such a loser don't know what to do…….booohooo!!!"
