for a very long time, companies and people believed in keeping their projects secret and then one day, all of a sudden, launching it to the world.

the reasons might be many and from many aspects like marketing, tradition, common beliefs or just human nature to keep things secret.

this is how most people used to work on their projects in pre-covid era.

but after the covid, unfortunately many people around the world were impacted because of job shortage, layoffs and global lockdowns.

this led to more people switching to online businesses.

more people started building apps and they needed new ways to promote their products.

so a new trend started — where builders started doing the opposite of what used to happen before.

they started talking about their product when they'd just conceptualized it.

they started sharing the code.

they started sharing their failures, small wins and hurdles.

they started sharing the money they'd made.

they started sharing these things publicly.

this trend not only helped them build a brand but also getting users, increasing followers and so on.

because they sound more real, authentic and trustworthy than those companies who keep everything behind a polished shiny curtain.

this is what separated them from those traditional companies who build an artificial image.

this trend was named **Build-In-Public**.

to build in public, all you need to do is:

- share your journey with the world. your wins, your losses, your learnings, your observation.
- you can share anything that doesn't hurt you and anyone else if you do so.
